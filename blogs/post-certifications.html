Building a Career Path: The Best Certifications for IT and Cybersecurity

CompTIA A+ to PenTest+, CCNA to CEH — mapping the certification landscape so you can chart the fastest, smartest path into cybersecurity.

Published October 22, 2025 · Author Wil Dennison · Read time 8 min read

In today's digital world, technology keeps everything running — from hospitals and schools to businesses and governments. Behind the scenes, IT and cybersecurity professionals protect these systems from outages, data breaches, and cyberattacks. Certifications are one of the best ways to build skills and gain credibility. They prove to employers that you're serious about your career and have the technical knowledge to get the job done.

Starting Strong — Foundational Certifications

CompTIA A+
If you're new to tech, A+ is the place to start. It covers computer hardware, troubleshooting, operating systems, and networking basics — preparing you for roles such as Help Desk Technician or IT Support Specialist. Employers value A+ because it shows you can handle real-world IT problems and provide effective user support.
CompTIA AI Essentials
Artificial intelligence is reshaping every part of the tech industry, including cybersecurity. AI Essentials introduces beginners to the fundamentals of AI and its impact on business and security — covering generative AI, prompt engineering, ethical considerations, and future trends. No prior experience is required, making it a great entry point for understanding how AI integrates into IT and cybersecurity.

Leveling Up — Infrastructure and Networking

Once you've mastered the basics, expand your technical foundation with certifications that focus on networking, systems, and cloud infrastructure.

CompTIA Network+
Teaches how computers and devices communicate — covering IP addressing, routing, switching, and network security. Ideal for anyone pursuing roles in IT infrastructure or support.
Cisco CCNA
Dives deeper into enterprise networking, automation, and security principles. Validates your ability to install, configure, and troubleshoot networks used across large organizations.
CompTIA Linux+
Focuses on the OS that powers most servers, cybersecurity tools, and cloud environments. Teaches system configuration, file management, and command-line navigation — essential skills for anyone moving into cybersecurity or cloud computing.
CompTIA Server+
Covers server installation, virtualization, storage, and troubleshooting. A strong choice for professionals responsible for managing enterprise hardware and virtual environments.
CompTIA Cloud+
Demonstrates your ability to manage and secure cloud environments across AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud. As organizations migrate to cloud computing, this certification helps you stand out as someone who can design scalable, secure solutions.

Securing the Future — Cybersecurity Certifications

Once you've established a foundation in IT and infrastructure, the next step is learning how to defend those systems.

CompTIA Security+
Introduces core cybersecurity concepts including risk management, encryption, access control, and incident response. Globally recognized and approved by the U.S. Department of Defense (DoD 8570) as a required certification for many government cybersecurity positions.
Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH)
Offered by EC-Council, CEH teaches you to think like an attacker to identify and fix vulnerabilities before they can be exploited. Ideal for those interested in ethical hacking, penetration testing, or red team operations.
CompTIA CySA+
Focuses on threat detection, analysis, and defensive security. Validates intermediate-to-advanced skills and prepares professionals for roles such as Security Analyst or Cybersecurity Engineer.
CompTIA PenTest+
Emphasizes offensive testing and vulnerability exploitation. Prepares professionals for Penetration Tester roles and validates hands-on skills in planning, scoping, and executing security assessments.

Your Certification Roadmap

Many professionals follow a natural progression through the certification landscape:

  • Foundation — Start with A+ or AI Essentials to build core IT knowledge
  • Infrastructure — Expand with Network+, Linux+, Server+, Cloud+, or CCNA
  • Security — Specialize with Security+, then advance to CEH, CySA+, or PenTest+
// Key Takeaway
Certifications give you more than skills — they give you direction. As AI, cloud computing, and automation continue to evolve, staying certified ensures your knowledge remains current and your career continues to move forward. The world of IT and cybersecurity never stands still. Keep learning, stay adaptable, and treat each certification as a stepping stone toward your next achievement.
